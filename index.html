about

Fundamentals before automation.

A short version of how I think about infrastructure, and where I am right now.

I became interested in infrastructure after troubleshooting and recovering a misconfigured server — and that's when I actually fell in love with infrastructure. While most of my coursework lives in theory, I spend my time in the terminal: spinning up EC2 instances, breaking and fixing Docker containers, and mapping how networks talk to each other.

My core stack right now is AWS, Linux system administration, Docker, Git/GitHub, and networking fundamentals. I'm not claiming to be an expert — I'm a builder deliberately choosing the unglamorous, fundamentals-first path.

Outside the terminal, I've represented my university at the Smart India Hackathon, served as a Student Mentor with NITI Aayog, and volunteered with the National Service Scheme — experiences that taught me to communicate technical ideas simply and stay accountable under pressure.
